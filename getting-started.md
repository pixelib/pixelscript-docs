# Getting started

What PixelScript is, where to get it, and the mental model you need before writing anything.

Read these three in order. They take about fifteen minutes and will save you considerably more than that.

1. [PixelScript overview](./overview.md) covers what the runtime actually is and why it exists.
2. [Download PixelScript](./getting-started-001-download.md) covers release channels and where builds live.
3. [How to think about a script](./getting-started-002-how-to-think-about-a-script.md) recalibrates your expectations, especially if you are coming from Java or Skript.

When you are done, continue to the [Tutorial](./tutorial.md) to get a server running.
