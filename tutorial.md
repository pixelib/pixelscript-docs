# Tutorial

The hands-on path from a bare server to a working, hot-reloading codebase.

1. [Setting up your work environment](./tutorial-001-setting-up-your-work-env.md) gets the plugin installed, activated, and your IDE autocompleting the Bukkit API.
2. [Configuration](./tutorial-002-configuration.md) walks the three config files and what each one controls.
3. [Scripts and script management](./tutorial-003-scripts.md) explains the load tree: root, watched and imported scripts, and how to lay out a project.

The third one is the important one. Almost every confusing thing about PixelScript stops being confusing
once the load tree clicks.

After that, the [API reference](./api.md) covers everything your scripts can call.
